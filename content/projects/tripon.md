## Project scope

TripOn aimed to remove fragmentation in travel planning by giving users one clear journey from inspiration to confirmed itinerary.

## Design decisions

- Clarified progression from browse to booking.
- Reduced cognitive load in itinerary comparisons.
- Used visual hierarchy to keep decisions focused and confident.
