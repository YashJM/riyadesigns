## Project scope

Zapp Wallet focused on making digital payments feel fast, transparent, and trustworthy across onboarding, transfer, and balance tracking journeys.

## Design focus

- Reduce complexity in common payment tasks.
- Keep transaction states visible and easy to understand.
- Build confidence with clear security and confirmation moments.
