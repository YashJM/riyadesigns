## Project scope

The existing website did not reflect the personality and quality of the restaurant. This redesign introduced stronger visual storytelling, cleaner navigation, and faster access to key actions.

## Impact intent

- Improve findability of menu categories.
- Increase confidence around ordering and contact actions.
- Align the digital experience with the in-store brand.
